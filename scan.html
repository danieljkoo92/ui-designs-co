Free site check

Find out what your site is actually doing.

Not an opinion about the colours. A real check of the things that decide whether people find you, whether they can call you, and whether an AI assistant can name you when someone asks for a business like yours.

What gets checked

Twenty-odd checks in four groups. No guesswork and no AI writing you a vague report — every one of these is either there on your page or it isn't.

Getting found on Google

What a search engine reads before deciding where to put you.

  • Page title, and whether it says what you do
  • The description shown under your name in results
  • One clear main heading, not none or five
  • Sitemap and robots file
  • Whether shared links show a preview
  • Images with descriptions attached

Working on a phone

Where roughly seven in ten of your customers are.

  • Built to scale to a phone screen
  • Secure, so browsers don't warn people off
  • Page weight — what has to download before anything shows
  • How fast your server actually answers

Turning visitors into calls

Being found is worthless if the page doesn't make contact obvious.

  • A tappable phone number, not one they must copy out
  • A number visible on the page at all
  • A form for people who won't call
  • An address or service area, so locals know you cover them

Ready to be recommended by AI

What decides whether an assistant can name you.

  • Business details in a format machines read directly
  • Opening hours published so they can be quoted
  • Question-and-answer content an assistant can lift an answer from
  • Your service area written in plain sentences, not implied

The check reads the public page at the address you give it, the same as any search engine would. Nothing is stored, and no report is emailed to you — the results appear on screen and that's it.
