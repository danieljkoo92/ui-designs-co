## 6. Text messaging

If Client's plan includes automated messaging to Client's own customers (appointment reminders, review requests):

- Designer builds the messaging and a working opt-out ("reply STOP").
- **Client is responsible for having each recipient's consent to be texted** and for honoring every opt-out.
- Client understands the federal TCPA provides statutory damages of $500 per message, up to $1,500 for willful violations, recoverable by the recipient.
- Client will not upload purchased, scraped or otherwise non-consented phone lists.

## 7. Accessibility

Designer builds toward WCAG 2.1 Level AA: keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled form fields, text alternatives, tested contrast, and respect for reduced-motion settings.

Accessibility also depends on content added after delivery — later photos need alt text, videos with speech need captions, PDFs have their own requirements. A build includes accessible construction; it does **not** include a formal third-party conformance audit, certification, or ongoing monitoring. Those are available, quoted separately:

Hosting, domains, email, payment processors and other third-party services are outside Designer's control and carry their own terms and uptime.

## 10. Cancelling

Client may walk away at the preview stage at no cost. Once the build has started, work completed to that point is billable; on cancellation before delivery, Client pays for work performed and owes nothing further.
