Questions

What people ask before they book.

How much does balayage cost at Vesper?

Balayage starts at $260 and includes a gloss and a blowout. Very long or very thick hair, or a big change from dark to light, can take more time and product, and we confirm the full price at your free consult before anything starts.

How long does a balayage appointment take?

Plan on three to four hours. Going several shades lighter can take a second session a few weeks later, which is kinder to your hair than forcing it in one day.

Do you cut curly hair dry?

Yes. Amara cuts curls dry, one curl at a time, so the shape is set by how your hair actually falls. Come with your hair clean, dry and worn the way you usually wear it.

Do I need a consultation before colour?

For any colour service we start with a free 15-minute consult. First-time colour clients also get a patch test at least 48 hours before the appointment.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us 24 hours notice to cancel or move an appointment. Cancelling inside 24 hours is charged at half the booked service.

How do I get to the studio?

We are a five-minute walk from the Bedford Av L stop. Street parking is metered until 7pm on weekdays.
